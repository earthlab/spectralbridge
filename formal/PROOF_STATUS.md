PROVED refers to exact-real mathematics, not every named Python function. Conditional theorems are mathematical proofs whose interpretation requires the listed hypotheses; coefficient validity, physical mixing and biological truth are never global axioms. Numerical and empirical statuses are deliberately separate.

Conformance test names below omit the `test_` prefix and live in `formal/tests/test_conformance.py`. Previous empirical evidence lives in P129/P130 as mapped in TRACEABILITY.md.
