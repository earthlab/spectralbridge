# Claims and scientific boundary

The inventory is **mathematical**, **conditional mathematical**, **numerical conformance**, **empirical evidence**, or **not established**. “Proved” means the named theorem was accepted by the pinned Lean kernel with no `sorry` or custom axioms, as recorded in checks; it does not mean Python, metadata, geometry, observations or ecology have been verified.

1. A fixed linear sensor map preserves arbitrary finite weighted sums. A fixed affine map preserves sums with weights totaling one; the exact offset-defect condition is recorded even without this hypothesis. Neither statement requires the sensor map to be injective.
2. Preserving a mixture's weights algebraically does **not** guarantee the weights are recoverable. Rank cannot increase under composition. A linear many-to-fewer-band map is noninjective on the whole source space, although it can remain injective on a particular mixture simplex. A proved two-to-one-band example destroys identifiability of two simplex components.
3. Nonnegative normalized weights preserve constants and coordinate bounds. Inline SpectralBridge instead divides by sum plus epsilon: the exact real constant output is `sum/(sum+epsilon) × constant`. Matrix linearity remains valid. No exact normalized-average claim is made for an arbitrary inline SRF.
4. Frozen positive correction factors preserve nonnegativity/sign. Scene/chunk-fitted SCS+C, optional NDVI-conditioned BRDF, percentile matching and clipping are not all globally linear. Two fixed diagonal factors commute, but scaling and offset need not; clipping and medians cannot generally move across mixing. Primary polygon/year spectra are medians, not means.
5. Simplex synthetic fractions give a convex-hull spectrum and exact algebraic reconstruction. The pair condition covers every implemented pair-grid value; all four implemented triple recipes are proved feasible over reals. Role/identity disjointness, random sampling and IEEE generation remain implementation/evidence claims.
6. Ideal FCLS has a minimizer for a nonempty finite simplex, convex objective, and uniqueness under simplex identifiability. The known-generating-parent ideal zero-residual case recovers its weights only under this condition. Python's strict-interior/rank/tie tolerances are kept separate.
7. MESMA's finite admitted search has a tolerance bound, not a guarantee of biological composition. Distinct singleton and pair class systems can fit exactly with different class fractions. Saved near-fit class-composition ranges are narrower than the entire mathematical feasible near-fit set; no exhaustive uncertainty claim follows.
8. With positive alpha the centered ridge Gram matrix is positive definite/invertible, its normal-equation coefficients are unique, and its regularized objective has that unique minimizer. Proper-only preprocessing is frozen; the unpenalized intercept is restored for prediction. Uniqueness of coefficients is not uniqueness of spectral inversion.
9. The ideal Euclidean simplex projection exists, is unique, returns valid fractions, fixes feasible inputs, is idempotent, and does not increase distance to a feasible truth. The Python sorting implementation is tested against exact examples; its complete finite-array algorithm/IEEE equivalence is not formally verified.
10. Conditional full-pipeline recovery requires every stage to preserve the generating mixture, the supplied references to represent those components, transformed identifiability and an ideal constrained optimizer. The actual median/data-dependent pipeline is not asserted to satisfy those hypotheses universally.
11. Lower spectral reconstruction error does not order abundance error; perfect expected fraction prediction does not imply an identifiable inverse. These are counterexamples, not empirical superiority claims for either estimator.

The current report supplies **sampled-domain numerical/empirical evidence**, not theorems: all ten category pairs differ in the sampled cohorts; full-NEON BA 85.2% for unseen polygons and 73.8% for held sites; Woody shrub held-site recall 46.3%; synthetic ridge120 RMSE .216/.269 versus MESMA15 .359/.362; same15 ridge .365/.387 loses that advantage; global-year ridge fails the prior. These numbers are evidence references, not constants in proofs. The source tables/report are unchanged.

**Not established:** physical correctness of packaged synthetic translation coefficients on real drone/actual Landsat pairs; universal cross-region/year recognition; absolute scale/purity of the historical library; contemporary biological-change truth; exhaustive mixture identifiability; real field fractional-cover recovery. Statistical separability, recognition accuracy, reconstruction and field abundance are four distinct claims.
