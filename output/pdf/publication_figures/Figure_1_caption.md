**Figure 1. An empirical polygon spectral library spans ecological groups and sensor representations.**
(A) Corrected NEON category medians and empirical 25–75% wavelength-wise intervals for all 4,807 polygon/year spectra. Colors and line styles identify five researcher cover categories throughout A, C and D. Shaded vertical regions are excluded atmospheric domains (1340–1450 and 1790–2000 nm); curves do not bridge gaps. Values retain stored corrected reflectance units because absolute library scaling is unresolved.
(B) Observational hierarchy and verified counts: 3,639 polygon/plant identities, 85,706 source pixel records, 2,496 identities with species recorded and 1,143 without, across 3 sites and 2 acquisition years. A polygon/year curve is the equal-observation median of polygon/flight spectra, themselves pixel medians. NIWO recurs across years; years are not independent plants.
(C) Centered, unscaled, wavelength-quadrature-weighted principal component analysis (PCA) on 360 common finite NEON coordinates; PC1/PC2 explain 70.9%/26.8% of weighted spectral variance. Color denotes category; shape denotes site. All observations and extreme values are retained. PCA is descriptive and not a significance test.
(D) One observed curve nearest each category median under the same wavelength metric, with its paired undarkened synthetic Operational Land Imager (OLI) values (diamonds) at validated physical band centers (443, 482, 561.4, 654.6, 864.7, 1608.9 and 2200.7 nm). Examples are selected deterministically; full identities are in representatives.csv. These are NEON-derived sensor representations, not independent satellite measurements. Category overlap, unequal sampling and potential species/acquisition confounding preclude interpreting these views as classification or population accuracy.
