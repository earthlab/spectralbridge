**Figure 2. Conditional ecological spectral contrasts largely survive reduction to synthetic OLI, with a separate real-Landsat pilot agreement check.**
(A) Corrected NEON standardized root-mean-square (RMS) contrasts, sqrt(sum w(β/s)²), for all 21 eligible inferred plant functional type (PFT) comparisons within four site/year cohorts matched on exact acquisition histories. D/E denote deciduous/evergreen; B/C denote broadleaf/conifer; S/T denote shrub/tree. These inferred PFTs differ from researcher cover categories. Dots denote Holm-adjusted synchronized maximum-plus-integrated nonparametric combination (NPC) p < 0.05 in the declared primary family; gray cells lack sufficient matched support. Exact polygon/year counts and p-values are in plotted_effects.csv.
(B) Signed block-centered standardized mean contrasts β/s for two previously examined strong comparisons at NIWO 2023. Thick segments exceed the canonical family-controlled simultaneous maximum null envelope (Bonferroni across 21 matched contrasts), not an effect confidence interval. OLI diamonds mark seven actual channel centers. Atmospheric domains remain disconnected; no pointwise uncertainty is promoted to simultaneous evidence.
(C) Paired NEON/OLI effects for the same 21 dependent contrast/cohort comparisons; marker shape and color encode support. 15/16 NEON-supported contrasts retain OLI support; NIWO 2023 DBS–DBT loses borderline support. NEON uses disconnected wavelength weights and OLI equal-channel weights: these effects and their ratios are representation-specific separation measures, not fractions of total ecological information or classification accuracy.
(D) One R10C acquisition pair: NEON 15 September 2021 and actual Landsat 8 Collection 2 Level-2 surface reflectance on 16 September. RGB uses red/green/blue, identical 0–0.4 stretch, full common extent, EPSG:32613 native 30 m cells and common validity mask (gray excluded). NIR is near-infrared; residual = synthetic minus real, colors bounded at ±0.15 with all values retained numerically. Metrics use all 3,583 accepted footprints (56 spatial blocks): pooled seven-band RMSE 0.0148, median spectral angle 2.09°, bandwise r 0.81–0.95. This separate flight is not a direct observation of the library polygons. The approximately 22.4 h lag is conditional on the legacy NEON UTC interpretation; one pilot and high correlation do not establish cross-site radiometric interchangeability, causal PFT mechanisms or mixture recoverability.
