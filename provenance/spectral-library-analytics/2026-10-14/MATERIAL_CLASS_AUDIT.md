Non-vegetated & dead is heterogeneous. Bare is not silently renamed pure soil. Mixed Dead / Bare / Rock remains a mixture label. Dead debris has one identity. Shade flags describe observation context, not a measured standalone shade endmember. Water, snow, verified pure soil/mineral and independent fractional-cover benchmarks are not established. Prior proxy-material experiments remain conditional diagnostics.

New controlled predictive/selected-band mixtures follow the two primary biological schemes; the earlier material branch remains limited. No operational MESMA fraction is approved because classification works.
