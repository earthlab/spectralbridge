# Observational structure and independence

Pixels are nested within a polygon/plant/flight/source observation. Wavelength-wise
pixel medians reduce that observation immediately. Equal observation medians form
GlobalID/year summaries. GlobalID remains the biological cluster across years and
all flights. Local polygon numbers are not globally independent identities.

The canonical index links site/year/GlobalID to compact NEON/OLI row keys, complete
nested-JSON flight/source histories, source-native grid identities and metadata.
Annual and actual flight targets are different; held-flight experiments must use
the latter, with all exposed identities purged from training. No duplicated source
pixel matrix is needed. Original-source and derivative hashes remain authoritative.

There are three sites and two years with incomplete crossing: NIWO is observed in
2020/2023; WREF/YELL in 2023. Cross-year transfer is not replicated temporal ecology
across every site. PFT, species, site and acquisition histories are partly aliased.
Rare/absent classes must be recorded, not collapsed or filled. The label systems
(species, inferred PFT, researcher category, physical-material proxy) remain separate.

Prediction fitting treats polygon/year rows as observations with shared-cluster
holdouts; uncertainty samples GlobalIDs. Domain diagnostics may use compact
plant/flight observations, still grouped by GlobalID. Mixture fraction trajectories
reuse empirical parents and are not independent plants at each fraction. Actual
Landsat comparison uses 30 m footprints and spatial blocks, not library polygon
classification or independent fractional-cover truth.
