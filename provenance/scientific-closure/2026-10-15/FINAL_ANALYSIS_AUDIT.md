## Redundant work explicitly not repeated

No new model zoo/deep architecture, broad hyperparameter search, PFT permutation family, exhaustive acquisition suite, endmember selector sweep, synthetic fraction generation, full-atlas rerender or real Landsat reprocessing. Existing signed contrasts, resolution curves, calibration and mixture outcomes are reused with their original limitations. Five synthesis figures replace a proliferation of new exploratory figures.

## Nonidentifiability and stop rule

Site/year/class/flight exposure are not a balanced crossed experiment. A site random-effect variance cannot be reliably identified from this observational three-site/two-year design; within NIWO there is no site variation at all. The paired balanced-panel SS decomposition is an equivalent descriptive quantity, not a causal population variance-component estimate. Flight histories can change between years, so paired changes combine temporal ecology and observation/processing conditions. Independent abundance/purity truth, verified library scale and MicaSense coordinates require new data rather than further fitting.


## Final metadata findings that limit ecological interpretation

The only repeated-year site is NIWO (1,168 paired polygon IDs). Source field annotations date from 2023/2024 and are reused across image years; NIWO polygon imagery_year remains 2020 for both 2020 and 2023 extractions. These properties are retained as annotation/delineation metadata, not overwritten or used as actual acquisition dates. Stable labels are not independent annual field confirmation or verified biological individual persistence. Paired classification is retrospective recognition of labelled footprints.

The metadata-only exact source-image/flight/row/column audit finds zero pixels shared by distinct GlobalIDs among 85,706 records, with no missing keys. Spatial autocorrelation, purity and possible unresolved geometric issues remain outside that check. All 122 source products have recoverable 1 m raster-coordinate vectors in metre grids; there are 14 site-date groups, not necessarily 14 independent flight events. Median extraction support is two pixels per polygon/acquisition (12,504 observations). This limits physical endmember/purity interpretation and highlights the gap from actual 30 m Landsat mixture footprints. Source spectral convolution does not change that spatial support.
