# Closure design fixed before new fits

The existing 708-fit, functional, sensor, mixture and real-Landsat audits are reused, with their hashes and limitations. No new algorithm search, mixtures, sensor coefficients or production normalization.

## Questions still requiring calculation

1. NIWO paired changes: complete GlobalID pairs, stable labels per resolution, full common NEON and paired OLI; stored intensity and unit-norm shape. Balanced two-year sum-of-squares identity separates class, polygon within class, common year, class-by-year and residual change. These are exact descriptive orthogonal partitions, not causal or identifiable regional random-effects estimates. Site variance is not estimable within NIWO; global site/year/class partitions are non-additive confounded descriptive associations. Two time points cannot establish a temporal population variance.
2. Biological resolution: recorded binomial species (exact two-token source names), researcher five-class cover, inferred narrow woody PFT, broader **leaf-habit** PFT (deciduous/evergreen within assigned woody taxa), coarse researcher cover proxy (vegetation labels versus heterogeneous nonvegetated/dead). These are partly parallel label systems on different populations, not an assumed strictly nested hierarchy or a claim that coarse means physically pure.
3. Paired temporal recognition: train a logistic model on earlier-year **other identities**, with grouped inner tuning and independent calibration as available; assess the same held-out identities in both years. Three identity folds, both directions where supported. This isolates temporal change among repeated polygons from the previously confounded global year holdouts. It does not establish an independent third-year forecast.
4. Broader/species/coarse recognition: reuse existing fold/fit utilities, minimum 10 training and 3 test identities per class, logistic only, common NEON/OLI, plant/site/year; no acquisition/model sweep. Existing researcher/narrow baseline fits are not repeated.
5. Brightness and weighting: on existing researcher/narrow primary NEON plant/site/year folds compare unit spectral norm (shape), a single norm coordinate (brightness only) and one-observation-per-training-identity (earliest year) against the saved baseline. Paired memberships and test targets fixed. Per-observation normalization has no fitted population parameters; train-only scaling/tuning remains inside CV. Production spectra stay unchanged.

## Uncertainty and decisions

499 seeded whole-identity resamples, stratified within biological classes; paired years and sensor/model comparisons use identical identity draws. Conditional intervals, not geographic-population confidence. Classification: balanced accuracy, class recall/precision/specificity/FPR/FNR, support and confusion; calibration and conformal diagnostics reuse established implementation. No new p-value family is introduced; exploratory intervals are not adjusted superiority tests. Paired descriptive class effects are not confirmatory claims.

Readiness is use-specific, with predeclared review aids rather than universal approval cutoffs: at least 10 training/3 held-test identities per class; conditional closed-set discrimination is 'scientifically promising' when fold BA lower bound exceeds its equal-class chance reference. 'Validated for constrained use' additionally requires every supported class recall >=.70, BA lower bound >=.70 in the specified tested domain, and explicit operational error tolerance/independent validation. No post-hoc best model is chosen. Operational abundance additionally requires independent measured fraction/purity truth, verified scale and calibrated uncertainty. Current missing truth blocks that category irrespective of synthetic RMSE. Ambiguity >.10 at the established .01 residual tolerance flags nonunique composition; synthetic fraction RMSE >.10 flags inadequate precision for a hypothetical ten-percentage-point application. These are transparent scenario thresholds, not physical laws. Unknown rejection review uses recall >=.80 at known FPR <=.05; show actual values and mark failure without optimizing thresholds on unknowns.

## Stop rule

After these calculations, make the final evidence ladder, readiness table, repeated-observation policy, five synthesis figures and one human report. No further analytical branch unless an error invalidates a required conclusion. Preserve all earlier evidence and the source library in place. GlobalID denotes a polygon identity; biological individual/population independence beyond that is unverified.

## Bounded clarification after first targeted results

The new broad woody leaf-habit classifier is a candidate central ecological result. The original shape/amplitude sensitivity covered the two existing label schemes but not this newly fitted broad label. Therefore extend **the same fixed sensitivity, without changing grids or thresholds**, to broad leaf habit: 16 NEON fits (shape/amplitude × three plant/three site/two year folds). This directly tests a required brightness-driven-discrimination concern before making the broad-group conclusion. No species/algorithm/sensor sweep or additional branch follows. Record closed-set target coverage so filtering absent/rare species cannot masquerade as all-taxon generalization.

The earliest-observation-per-ID sensitivity jointly changes training representation and weighting; it cannot isolate a pure weighting effect. Exact sampling shares quantify repeat overweighting separately. Recommended inverse-observation-count identity weights preserve all valid repeated spectra, but are a sampling-policy recommendation, not a newly validated production default.
