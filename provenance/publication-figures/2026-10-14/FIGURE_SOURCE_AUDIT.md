## Repository assessment and design changes

Existing atlas/year utilities establish pixel → polygon/flight median → equal-observation polygon/year median. Existing harmonization interpolates native grids only over supported short intervals, never atmospheric gaps. The merged union is structural missingness, not a rectangular analytical wavelength matrix. Prior quantile outputs are fitted descriptive summaries, not independent observations; they are intentionally not used as empirical replicates. Researcher-category results provide broad complementary evidence but are not added to the primary inferred-PFT matrix. The MESMA audit is background on interpretation limits, not a main-figure panel.

Absolute physical scale for the polygon library is not established by retained source metadata. Keep stored units and label honestly; do not divide by 10,000 on appearance. Pilot SR has independently verified scaling. Primary atlas shows empirical IQR and median, not thousands of overplotted raw pixels; bounds contain every median/IQR point and do not change analytical arrays. PCA retains extreme spectra and exports full scores/memberships so leverage remains inspectable. No geography map without verified locations is invented.

Final manuscript size chosen 7.2 inches (182.88 mm) wide, at most 6.65 inches (168.91 mm) high; 400 dpi raster export, editable SVG text / embedded TrueType PDF. Reference: [Nature figure sizing/export guide](https://research-figure-guide.nature.com/figures/building-and-exporting-figure-panels/), accessed 2026-10-14 (183 mm double column, 170 mm maximum height). Requested 6.5–9 pt labels take precedence over any journal-specific smaller lettering; exact target-journal formatting remains editorial review.
