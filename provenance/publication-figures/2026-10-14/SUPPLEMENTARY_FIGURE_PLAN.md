# Recommended supplementary figures (not generated automatically)

1. Sampling and QA: site × year × researcher category × species counts; identity repeats; shade annotations; native wavelength grids, structural missingness and extreme-value provenance. Recover source physical units before final absolute-reflectance labels.
2. Complete statistical evidence: all supported/unsupported matched comparisons, exact n, NPC/max/integrated probabilities, scalar-family sensitivity, alternative conditioning, empirical null calibration and full simultaneous envelopes. Treat family null envelopes separately from effect intervals.
3. Researcher categories: their separate 40-comparison analysis, including mixed nonliving cover, with all support/size tables. Do not merge these labels with inferred PFTs.
4. Sensor-transfer diagnostics: paired cluster-bootstrap uncertainty, OLI equal-channel versus FWHM weights, effect ratios, contrast ranking, projected signed-band consistency, SWIR ablation and site-specific directions. Ratios are metric-specific, not literal information fractions.
5. Real-Landsat pilot: bandwise scatter, identity/Deming fits, Bland–Altman residuals, spatial-block intervals, all QA/coverage masks, edge/heterogeneity effects, failed pixels, registration offsets and paired brightness-adjustment results. Preserve all outliers. Resolve timezone and historic coefficient-training provenance; add independent flights/sites before a general validation claim.
6. Morphospace robustness: compare all-data PCA with explicit brightness/shape decompositions and source-grid/site coloring only after scientific choices are justified; no post hoc clipping to separate classes. Current all-data PCA is descriptive, not an optimized classifier.
7. Quantile curves and MESMA: separate future figures. Quantile fits are models, not new empirical endmembers; separability does not establish identifiability or fraction accuracy. The existing MESMA audit should retain missing purity/units/material/independent-ground-truth gates.
