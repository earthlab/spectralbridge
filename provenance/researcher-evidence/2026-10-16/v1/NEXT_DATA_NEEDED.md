# What data should be collected next

1. Independent contemporaneous field fractions for all five recorded categories,
   at explicitly matched spatial/PSF support. Include true zeros, low, intermediate
   and high/pure-end support, measurement uncertainty and held-out sites. Keep these
   observations outside all library selection, tuning and calibration.
2. Verify reflectance units/scaling and radiometric lineage; independently assess
   polygon/material purity and actual mixing linearity. Synthetic OLI convolution
   is not independent satellite validation and does not change native spatial support.
3. Cross more sites with more years, balance category support and independently
   reannotate repeated biological observations. Include spatially separated or
   buffered targets to address neighborhood autocorrelation.
4. Deliberately collect difficult Woody shrub/Herbaceous/Deciduous overlaps and
   heterogeneous backgrounds. Validate absence detection and low-fraction recovery
   separately rather than assuming the declared .10 comparison rule is physical.
5. Freeze the next external validation design, including retained parent/library
   capacity and expected fraction prior. The same-candidate result means model
   capacity and independent parent diversity require explicit controls.

Do not replace these missing observations with more mixtures from the same parents.
