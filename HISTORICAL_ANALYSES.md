# Historical spectral-library workflows

The current primary biological analysis is the recorded researcher-category
workflow at `examples/researcher_pft_information.py`, with its versioned evidence
in `provenance/researcher-pft-information/2026-10-15/v1/`.

Older species-derived grouping workflows and mixed-label summaries are retained
for historical reproduction. They are **not current primary analyses**:

- `examples/plant_spectral_pfts.py`, `plant_pft_statistics.py` and the original
  PFT method-audit workflow;
- the original dual-label sensor-transfer, MESMA-readiness and integrated
  spectral-library analytics experiments;
- `examples/publication_figures/` dual-label ecological panels;
- dated `provenance/scientific-closure/` and `provenance/lab-story/` report builders
  and their earlier PDFs;
- taxonomic crosswalk metadata and notebook cells that reproduce those branches.

These original implementations/builders are frozen rather than rewritten to
make old results appear researcher-derived. Generic numerical helpers remain
reusable with explicit recorded-category labels. Historical tests/fixtures may
retain the earlier codes. Source data and previous results were not deleted.

The current root FINAL_* pointers and primary website pages use only the five
recorded cover categories. The complete textual inventory, changed primary
surfaces, historical snapshot copies and active/historical registry are in
`provenance/researcher-pft-information/2026-10-15/v1/LABEL_SYSTEM_AUDIT.md`.
