# Anatomy of a Red Artifact, Part II

The November 2026 companion explains numerical encoding, observation eligibility,
reconstructed geometry, experimental BRDF stability and ecological evidence as
separate steps. Its complete illustrated PDF, editable Markdown/page manifest,
25 PNG/SVG figures and 157-row provenance ledger are in
`provenance/brdf-artifact-anatomy-part-ii/2026-11-04/` in the repository.

The controlled corrected eligibility population is 6,678,404 pixels, the clean
zero-view source population is 6,683,944, and the reconstructed terrain-valid
domain is 6,683,905. Correct filtering does not repair rank-one fixed geometry.
All four inferred viewing designs reach numerical rank three, but true
contributors and physical calibration remain unverified.

The fixed polygon median spectra include within-polygon IQR. Classification uses
mean spectra on frozen folds and 157 identities; its paired uncertainty does not
show a decisive gain. The separate integrated-reliability investigation retains
SCS+C with explicit unsupported-illumination classification and reports native
and common-support results separately. Neither report promotes experimental
products to production.
