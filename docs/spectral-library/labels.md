# Researcher categories are the canonical biological grouping

The primary spectral-library analysis uses the source **`cover_category`** field,
verbatim, with these five researcher-defined groups:

- Deciduous
- Evergreen
- Herbaceous
- Non-vegetated & dead
- Woody shrub

“Researcher PFT” and “researcher cover category” refer to these recorded labels
throughout the current analysis. The population includes all 3,639 polygon IDs,
including identities without a species name. Species metadata are provenance;
no species lookup defines the primary classes.

**Non-vegetated & dead is heterogeneous, not a certified pure endmember.** A
polygon annotation does not measure pure material composition or complete
fractional cover. The biological sampling unit is GlobalID/polygon; it is not
a pixel, an independent wavelength or necessarily one biological individual.
Field annotations from 2023/2024 reused across imagery years are not independent
annual biological verification.

Earlier species-derived woody groupings and their reports are historical and
exploratory. They are preserved for provenance, excluded from the new primary
analysis and not interchangeable with researcher categories. The explicit
[active/historical audit](https://github.com/earthlab/spectralbridge/blob/main/provenance/researcher-pft-information/2026-10-15/v1/LABEL_SYSTEM_AUDIT.md)
records this boundary. No source data or historical outputs were deleted.
